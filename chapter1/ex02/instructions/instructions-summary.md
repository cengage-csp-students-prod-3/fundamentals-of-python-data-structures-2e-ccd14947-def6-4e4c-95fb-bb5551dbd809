Compute an employee's total weekly pay.


Useful facts:
     An employee's total weekly pay equals the
     hourly wage multiplied by the total number
     of regular hours plus any overtime pay.  Overtime
     pay equals the total overtime hours multiplied by 1.5
     times the hourly wage.


Write a program that takes `float` inputs of:

1. The hourly wage

2. Total regular hours

3. Total overtime hours

4. Displays an employee's total weekly pay.

> Total weekly pay should have no more than 2 decimal places. 

Here is a sample interaction with this program:
```
Enter the wage: $30
Enter the regular hours: 40
Enter the overtime hours: 5
The total weekly pay is $1425.0
```
